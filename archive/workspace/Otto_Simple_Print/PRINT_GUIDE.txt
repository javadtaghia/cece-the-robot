Simple Otto DIY V9 print set

Print quantities:
- OttoDIY_head_V9.stl: 1
- OttoDIY_body_V9.stl: 1
- OttoDIY_Leg_V9.stl: 2

Use millimetres and 100% scale in your slicer.
