# Cece carrier R15: screenshot-right shoulder moves outward

Current revision. The user confirmed a sideways movement of the shoulder on the right of the supplied view. That end is negative X in the saved assembly. R14 was an unpublished opposite-end trial and is superseded; use R15.

- Shoulder centre shifts from X=-10.15 to -13.15 mm.
- Inner face shifts from X=-8.15 to -11.15 mm, giving 3.5 mm nominal side clearance from the board end at X=-7.65 mm.
- The opposite shoulder stays at X=16.15 mm.
- The connecting rear span extends to meet the shifted shoulder. A return along the front of the cradle reconnects it outside the board access zone, keeping the carrier one piece.
- Electronics, USB-first insertion, camera contact and wall supports retain their R13 positions.

## Checks

Comparison against R13 confirms all carrier changes are at the requested negative-X end. Board, USB socket, camera housing and lens shapes and coordinates are unchanged. No carrier material remains in the widened side-access zone. New route has no intersection with source assembly components or clearance allowances. Existing head, ultrasonic, USB and sampled insertion checks pass. One valid solid and closed STL.

Physical pin geometry and stiffness remain unverified. The 3.5 mm figure is side clearance to the board envelope, not a measured clearance to actual pins.
