Cece R5 deeper slide-in ESP carrier - review before printing

Corrected direction: increase front-to-back rail depth by 5 mm, from
5.4 to 10.4 mm. Do not lift the board or change its camera aperture height.
Stacked ESP/camera position is restored to R3. The central crossbar stays
narrow and open, and the 20 mm side grips are retained.

PCB-edge channels allow sliding in from the open USB end, with a closed
far-end stop. Load the ESP into the carrier OUTSIDE the head, then install
the loaded carrier. Lateral insertion while the lens projects through the
head aperture is not possible with the current round camera hole.
This is not a separate removable sled and has no locking latch.

Channel gap 2.2 mm assumes 1.6 mm PCB thickness plus 0.3 mm clearance on
each face. Real PCB thickness, edge components and print tolerance need
checking before claiming reliable capture or retention.

CAD checks: one valid solid, closed STL. Sampled 26 mm rail insertion
travel at 0.5 mm increments has no collision with the modeled PCB, USB,
camera housing or lens, outside the head. These are provisional proxies;
the full stack cuboid is not a component-accurate collision model.
No unintended head collision at final pose. Minimum ultrasonic PCB gap
5.4 mm. Existing nominal side contact interference remains intentional.
Loaded carrier insertion into head and physical grip are unverified.
Solid-volume PLA estimate 3.15 g, before slicing.

R5 has not been sent to the printer. Prior revisions remain available.
