# Cece ESP carrier R8

The user confirmed moving the entire ESP holder 10 mm in the photo arrow direction. The cradle and ESP module move 10 mm toward the USB end (+X in CAD); the wall grips and camera opening stay fixed. This models the camera displaced 10 mm toward the opposite end relative to the module centre. Previous versions are preserved.

## Upper camera flap

A 6 mm wide, 0.8 mm thick hanging leaf connects to the upper support through an overhead arm. A 5 x 3 mm contact pad bears on the rear of the camera housing, not on the lens. The undeformed pad has 0.20 mm intentional interference with the provisional housing, representing the proposed elastic preload. Contact force, fatigue and ribbon clearance remain physically unverified. Do not force the assembly if the pad touches the cable or electronics.

## Loading and checks

The shifted cradle shortens the free space on the USB side. A loading tunnel has therefore been cut into the rear section of that side grip and upper bar. The front wall-contact pad remains in place. Head grip strength after this notch requires a fit check.

Load the module into the carrier outside the head, gently holding the flap away, then install the loaded carrier. Sampled 23.5 mm side-loading travel in 0.5 mm increments has no intersections with the fixed carrier. This test excludes the flexible flap; it is not a deformation simulation. No positive latch has been verified.

Checks pass: one valid solid, closed STL, zero unintended modeled head or ultrasonic overlap. Minimum carrier-to-ultrasonic-PCB distance is 4.8 mm. Intentional contact is limited to the head pads and camera pad. Approximate solid-volume PLA mass is 2.57 g, before slicing/supports.

The module remains 21.3 x 18 x 4.5 mm. Channel dimensions remain 18.6 x 5.1 mm. Exact camera housing, ribbon routing and the 2 mm vertical lens-to-module-centre offset remain provisional. Physical assembly is the final fit check.
