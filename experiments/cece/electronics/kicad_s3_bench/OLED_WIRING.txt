CECE OLED - COMBINED SCHEMATIC REVISION 1.4

Selected display: Adafruit 4741, grayscale 1.5 inch 128 x 128 SSD1327,
STEMMA QT / Qwiic breakout. These connections are for this module.

OLED terminal       ESP32-S3 Sense terminal      Shared with driver
VIN                 3V3                         3V3 LOGIC
GND                 GND                         GND LOGIC
DATA / SDA          D4 / GPIO5                  SDA
CLK / SCL           D5 / GPIO6                  SCL

Use a four-wire 1 mm JST-SH STEMMA QT / PiicoDev-compatible cable from
an unused I2C socket on the servo driver to either OLED QT socket.
Match connector pin functions; do not infer pin order from block positions.
Both OLED QT sockets are wired in parallel; only one is needed.
VIN takes 3.3 V here, keeping the shared I2C pull-ups at ESP logic voltage.
Never connect the servo 5 V supply to the driver's I2C power connector.
Do not use the OLED 3Vo output as its power input.

Leave OLED J1 and J2 closed for I2C (factory configuration).
Leave A0 open: default address 0x3D. Bridging A0 changes it to 0x3C.
Servo driver address 0x44 with its two address switches OFF.
The 4-wire QT mode does not require a separate reset GPIO. Leave RST,
CS, DC/A0 and 3Vo header pins externally unconnected for this setup.
Do not add SPI wiring. The symbol shows the four connected QT functions.

Firmware setup: Wire.begin(5, 6); use the Adafruit_SSD1327 library,
128 x 128, I2C address 0x3D, reset pin -1 for the QT connection.
Start at 100 kHz while checking the shared bus. Scan for 0x3D and 0x44.
This schematic change does not add or validate display firmware.
No physical OLED test, regulator load test or I2C rise-time measurement
has been performed. Verify operation alongside camera, Wi-Fi and servos.

Checks: KiCad ERC and exported-net checks include OLED power, ground,
SDA and SCL and preservation of ultrasonic and servo connections.
Main schematic: Cece_USB_Bench_Wiring.kicad_sch (single combined sheet).
Detailed power/charging notes: POWER_AND_CHARGING.txt.
