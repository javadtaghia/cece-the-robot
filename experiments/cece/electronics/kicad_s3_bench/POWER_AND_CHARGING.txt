POWER / PROGRAMMING MODES
RUN: no computer USB; JP1 fitted; switch ON.
Battery -> switch -> UBEC 5V -> servos AND ESP.
ESP branch: JP1 -> D1 -> ESP 5V header.
PROGRAM: switch OFF; REMOVE JP1; then plug USB.
After programming: unplug USB, then refit JP1.
D1 alone does NOT prevent PC backfeed; remove JP1.
Never connect 2S pack directly to ESP 5V or BAT+.
All GND labels share ground. Use proper power wire.

BATTERY HAS TWO SEPARATE CONNECTORS
Main 2-wire lead: RED = pack +; BLACK = pack -.
Main lead supplies the switch / UBEC as drawn above.
3-wire BLACK / BLUE / RED plug = 2S balance lead.
Balance functions: pack -, cell midpoint, pack +.
Wire-to-pin order is NOT verified: do not wire by colour.
Midpoint is NOT ground; never join it to GND.

CHARGER CONNECTION - PENDING IDENTIFICATION
Unplug MAIN lead from robot before charging.
Use a charger explicitly rated for 2S LiPo (8.4V full).
Balance plug -> charger 2S balance socket, after pin check.
Some chargers ALSO need main red/black charge leads;
others charge through the balance plug alone.
Exact connections require charger model + plug photos.
Do NOT use an unverified Adafruit 1S USB-C charger.
ESP USB and UBEC do not charge this pack.
