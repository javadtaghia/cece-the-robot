Cece: XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense + PiicoDev CE09181 + four SG90 servos

FIRST BENCH TEST: TWO SEPARATE POSITIVE POWER SUPPLIES, COMMON GROUND

PC USB-C ------------------------> XIAO ESP32-S3 USB-C
XIAO 3V3 --------------------------------> PiicoDev logic 3V3
XIAO GND --------------------------------> PiicoDev logic GND
XIAO D4 / GPIO5 --------------------------> PiicoDev SDA
XIAO D5 / GPIO6 --------------------------> PiicoDev SCL

Dualsky 2S battery -> Pololu Mini PB Reverse switch -> Hobbywing UBEC INPUT
Battery + / red -------------------------------> switch VIN
Switch VOUT -----------------------------------> UBEC IN +
Battery - / black ------------------------------> switch GND and UBEC IN -

SWITCH: POLOLU MINI PUSHBUTTON POWER SWITCH WITH REVERSE VOLTAGE PROTECTION
This is the LV #2808 / POLOLU-2808 ordered for this prototype. Follow the PCB
VIN/VOUT/GND labels, not an assumed left-to-right pin position.
Press its onboard button once for on, again for off. Leave ON, OFF, CTRL and
external-button control pads unconnected for this manual test.
The switch goes BEFORE the UBEC; it does not set the UBEC output voltage.
The ESP remains on while powered by PC USB even when this switch is off.
Later, with the ESP powered from a downstream UBEC branch and USB unplugged,
the button switches the battery-powered robot together.
Reverse-voltage protection is not a charger, fuse or LiPo low-voltage cutoff.
The electronic switch does not provide complete electrical isolation when
off. Unplug the battery before rewiring, for a jam, or before charging.

UBEC OUTPUT: SET TO 5.0 V AND MEASURE BEFORE CONNECTING
UBEC OUT + / red -------------------------------> PiicoDev servo power 5V
UBEC OUT - / black -----------------------------> PiicoDev servo power GND/GNDPWR

Place a 1000uF / 25V electrolytic capacitor close to the servo power input:
capacitor + to the 5V rail; capacitor negative/striped side to GND/GNDPWR.

The PiicoDev board connects logic GND and servo-power GND on the PCB.
The logic GND cable therefore joins ESP ground to the UBEC ground.
Route servo supply current through the power breakout and power wires, not
through the thin PiicoDev signal cable, ESP board, or breadboard contacts.
During this USB-powered ESP test, DO NOT connect UBEC OUT + to the XIAO.

PIICODEV CONNECTION OPTIONS
For logic, use its labelled GND/3V3/SDA/SCL breakout OR the PiicoDev breadboard
adapter and cable. Follow labels; do not infer connector order from wire colours.
For motor power, use its labelled 5V/GND power breakout (J6/J7 on published Rev10).
Use suitable soldered headers/pigtails or terminals and insulate joints.
The two PiicoDev USB-C connectors share the servo rail. Never connect a PC or
USB power supply there at the same time as UBEC power.
Do not connect the raw 2S battery to PiicoDev 5V, ESP 5V, ESP 3V3 or ESP BAT.

SERVO PLUGS
Brown/black -> GND. Red -> 5V. Orange/yellow -> signal/PWM.
Confirm the actual servo lead colours and PCB silk before applying power.
Socket 1: left hip/body servo; internal PCA9685 channel 3.
Socket 2: right hip/body servo; internal channel 2.
Socket 3: left foot servo; internal channel 1.
Socket 4: right foot servo; internal channel 0.
Both address switches OFF: I2C address 0x44.
No direct ESP-to-servo signal wires are used with this controller.
The Sense camera/microphone stay connected as before; GPIO5/6 are free.

TEST SEQUENCE
1. Unplug USB and battery before wiring. Support the robot and remove horn
   loads for first centering: 1500 us is an assumed electrical centre, not
   a measured physical zero. A newly enabled servo can move significantly.
2. Connect the logic wiring only. Leave battery/motor power disconnected.
3. Connect ESP USB, upload S3MediaServo/S3MediaServo.ino with XIAO_ESP32S3,
   Hardware CDC + JTAG, USB CDC Enabled, 8MB flash, OPI PSRAM.
4. Open Cece's existing phone page. Wi-Fi configuration uses the same saved
   settings as S3PhoneMedia. Rescan should detect the controller at 0x44.
   Detection tests logic communication only; it does not prove motor power.
5. With the controller disconnected from the UBEC output, set UBEC to 5.0V.
   Connect battery to UBEC input; measure OUT + to OUT - with a multimeter.
   Disconnect battery, connect the verified 5V output to the driver, reconnect.
6. Plug in one unloaded servo. Check supply polarity. Confirm the page's
   measured-5V/unloaded checkbox, then press that socket's test button.
7. Each test sends centre, then a smooth 1400..1600 us movement, returns to
   centre, and disables its signal after about 1.9 seconds. It does not walk.
8. Test each servo separately, then repeat with all four plugged in, one at a
   time. Run camera + microphone streams while repeating the tests.
9. Check voltage at the servo board during movement. A multimeter can miss
   short dips; resets/video dropouts need a power/connection investigation.
10. STOP disables PWM signals; it is not a battery disconnect. Unplug the
    battery if a servo binds or communication fails.
11. Disconnect battery after the bench test. This firmware has no battery
    voltage sensing or automatic LiPo low-voltage cutoff.

BATTERY-ONLY OPERATION (AFTER THE USB-POWERED TEST WORKS)
Disconnect the PC USB cable. Keep the 5V UBEC branch to the servo controller.
A separate UBEC 5V branch may power XIAO 5V through a series 1N5819 Schottky:
UBEC +5V -> diode unbanded end -> diode banded end -> XIAO 5V.
UBEC ground -> XIAO GND. Keep logic 3V3 supplied by the XIAO regulator.
Do not reconnect a normal powered PC USB cable while this external 5V branch
is connected. A USB/external power-mux or verified isolation design is needed
for convenient simultaneous operation; the single diode is not a complete mux.
Check the voltage at XIAO 5V under Wi-Fi/camera load after the diode drop.

CHARGING: CHARGER MODEL STILL UNCONFIRMED
The Hobbywing UBEC is NOT a charger. XIAO's onboard charger is for a single
3.7V lithium cell; it cannot charge the Dualsky 7.4V 2S pack.
Use a charger specified for 2S LiPo balance charging (8.4V full pack voltage).
For an adjustable compatible charger, 0.5A is a conservative initial current
for this 550mAh pack, subject to the battery/charger instructions.
Disconnect the battery from the robot for this prototype charging procedure.
Charger main/balance-port connections depend on its exact model and the
battery plugs. Do not guess them or connect a bare USB socket to the battery.
