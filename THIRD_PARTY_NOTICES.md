# Third-party material and provenance

Cece includes and builds on materials from other projects. The Apache-2.0
`LICENSE` at the repository root does not replace the licenses applicable
to third-party files or resolve the licensing of every inherited design.
Please check file provenance and the applicable upstream terms before
redistribution or commercial use.

- Otto DIY reference files and derived designs are identified in
  [the Otto reference README](experiments/otto/otto_reference/README.md).
  That document states different licenses for Otto software, hardware and
  documentation and provides the attribution. The selected upstream source
  revision is also recorded in [the Cece study notes](experiments/cece/README.md).
- The PiicoDev servo-driver reference includes its
  [license](experiments/cece/packaging_study/reference/piicodev_servo_driver_LICENSE.md).
- The Adafruit display CAD reference includes its
  [license](experiments/cece/packaging_study/reference/adafruit_cad_parts_LICENSE.txt).
- The Wokwi PCA9685 chip model includes its
  [license](experiments/cece/firmware/CeceBench/chips/LICENSE.txt).
- The copied [earlier licensing notes](provenance/xgoduck-source-licensing.md)
  record the source project's previous licensing and provenance status. They
  are retained as historical evidence, not presented as a new blanket license.
